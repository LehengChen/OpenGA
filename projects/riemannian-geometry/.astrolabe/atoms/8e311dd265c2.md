title: parallel vector field
---
Let $M$ be a differentiable manifold with an affine connection $\nabla$. A vector
  field $V$ along a curve $c:I\to M$ is called \emph{parallel} when $\frac{DV}{dt}=0$ for
  all $t\in I$.
