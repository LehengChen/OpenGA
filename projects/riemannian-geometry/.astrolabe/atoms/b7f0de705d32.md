The hypothesis $K\ge\delta>0$ cannot be relaxed to $K>0$. Indeed, the paraboloid
  $\{(x,y,z)\in\mathbf{R}^3;z=x^2+y^2\}$ has curvature $K>0$, but is complete and
  non-compact.
