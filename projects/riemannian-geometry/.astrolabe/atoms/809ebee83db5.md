title: totally geodesic criterion
---
An immersion $f:M\to\overline{M}$ is geodesic at $p\in M$ if and only if every
geodesic $\gamma$ of $M$ starting from $p$ is a geodesic of $\overline{M}$ at $p$.

*Proof.* Let $\gamma(0)=p$, $\gamma'(0)=x$. Let $N$ be a local extension, normal to
$M$, of a vector $\eta$ normal to $M$ at $p$, and $X$ a local extension of
$\gamma'(t)$ to a tangent field on $M$. Since $\langle X,N\rangle=0$, at $p$,

$$
H_\eta(x,x)=\langle S_\eta(x),x\rangle=-\langle\overline{\nabla}_X N,X\rangle=-X\langle N,X\rangle+\langle N,\overline{\nabla}_X X\rangle=\langle N,\overline{\nabla}_X X\rangle.
$$

Thus $f$ is geodesic at $p$ iff for all $x\in T_pM$ the geodesic $\gamma$ tangent
to $x$ at $p$ satisfies: $\overline{\nabla}_X X(p)$ has no normal component;
equivalently, every geodesic $\gamma$ of $M$ starting from $p$ is a geodesic of
$\overline{M}$ at $p$. $\square
