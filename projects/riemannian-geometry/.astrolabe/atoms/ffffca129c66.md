title: curvature on a parametrized surface
---
Let $f:A\subset\mathbb{R}^2\to M$ be a parametrized surface (cf. Def. 3.3, Chapter
  3) and let $(s,t)$ be the usual coordinates of $\mathbb{R}^2$. Let $V=V(s,t)$ be a
  vector field along $f$. Then
  
  $$
  \frac{D}{dt}\frac{D}{ds}V-\frac{D}{ds}\frac{D}{dt}V=R\Big(\frac{\partial f}{\partial s},\frac{\partial f}{\partial t}\Big)V.\qquad(3)
  $$
