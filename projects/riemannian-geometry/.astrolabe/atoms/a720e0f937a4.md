title: change of parameters as an axiom

\notready
  
  Comparison with the definition of a regular surface in $\mathbb{R}^3$ shows that
  the essential point (besides the change of dimension from $2$ to $n$) was to
  isolate the fundamental property of the change of parameters — a \emph{theorem} for
  surfaces in $\mathbb{R}^3$ — and incorporate it as condition (2) of \cref{def:dc-ch0-2-1}. This is the condition that lets all of the ideas of differential calculus
  in $\mathbb{R}^n$ carry over to differentiable manifolds.
