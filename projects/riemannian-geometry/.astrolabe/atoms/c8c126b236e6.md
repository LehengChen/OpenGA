title: properties of the bracket

If $X,Y,Z$ are differentiable vector fields on $M$, $a,b\in\mathbb{R}$, and $f,g$
differentiable functions, then:

(a) $[X,Y]=-[Y,X]$ *(anticommutativity)*;

(b) $[aX+bY,Z]=a[X,Z]+b[Y,Z]$ *(linearity)*;

(c) $[[X,Y],Z]+[[Y,Z],X]+[[Z,X],Y]=0$ *(Jacobi identity)*;

(d) $[fX,gY]=fg[X,Y]+fX(g)Y-gY(f)X$.

*Proof.* (a),(b) are immediate. For (c), expand
$[[X,Y],Z]=XYZ-YXZ-ZXY+ZYX$ and
$[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]=XYZ-XZY-YZX+ZYX+YZX-YXZ-ZXY+XZY$; the second members
agree, and (c) follows using (a). For (d),
$[fX,gY]=fX(gY)-gY(fX)=fgXY+fX(g)Y-gfYX-gY(f)X=fg[X,Y]+fX(g)Y-gY(f)X$. $\square
