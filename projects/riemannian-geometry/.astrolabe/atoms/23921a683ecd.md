title: minimizing geodesic
---
A segment of a geodesic $\gamma:[a,b]\to M$ is \emph{minimizing} if
  $\ell(\gamma)\le\ell(c)$ for every piecewise differentiable curve $c$ joining
  $\gamma(a)$ to $\gamma(b)$.
