title: symmetric connection

An affine connection $\nabla$ on a smooth manifold $M$ is said to be \emph{symmetric}
  when
  
  $$
  \nabla_X Y-\nabla_Y X=[X,Y]\quad\text{for all }X,Y\in\mathcal{X}(M).\qquad(5)
  $$
