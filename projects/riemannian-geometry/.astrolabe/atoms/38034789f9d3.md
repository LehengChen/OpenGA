The \emph{curvature tensor}
  
  $$
  R:\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)\to\mathcal{D}(M)
  $$
  
  is defined by
  
  $$
  R(X,Y,Z,W)=\langle R(X,Y)Z,W\rangle,\quad X,Y,Z,W\in\mathcal{X}(M).
  $$
  
  It is easy to verify that $R$ is a tensor of order 4, whose components in the
  frame $\{X_i=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}\}$ associated with the system of
  coordinates $(x_i)$ are $R(X_i,X_j,X_k,X_\ell)=R_{ijk\ell}$.
