If $M$ has a symmetric connection and $s:A\to M$ is a parametrized surface, then
  
  $$
  \frac{D}{\partial v}\frac{\partial s}{\partial u}=\frac{D}{\partial u}\frac{\partial s}{\partial v}.
  $$
