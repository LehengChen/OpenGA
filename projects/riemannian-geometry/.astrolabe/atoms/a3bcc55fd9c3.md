title: Levi-Civita
---
Given a Riemannian manifold $M$, there exists a unique affine connection $\nabla$
  on $M$ satisfying the conditions:
  - a) $\nabla$ is symmetric.
  - b) $\nabla$ is compatible with the Riemannian metric.
