title: existence and uniqueness of the bracket field
---
Let $X,Y$ be differentiable vector fields on $M$. Then there exists a unique
  vector field $Z$ such that $Zf=(XY-YX)f$ for all $f\in\mathcal{D}$.
  (Existence of $Z$ is the field $[X,Y]=$ \texttt{mlieBracket}; its smoothness is
  \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2-smooth}. The \emph{uniqueness} clause is the injective
  direction of the vector-field$\leftrightarrow$derivation correspondence,
  machine-checked as \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2-unique}. The \emph{existence
  characterization} $[X,Y]f=(XY-YX)f$ tying the bracket to the commutator of
  derivations at the manifold level is machine-checked as
  \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2-commutator} (on a boundaryless manifold), completing the
  formalized content of the lemma together with the narrow slices
  \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2-smooth}, \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2-unique},
  \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-3-antisymm}, \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-3-jacobi}.)
