title: normalization of a partition of unity
---
Let $\{f_\alpha\}$ be a differentiable partition of unity on $M$ subordinate to a
  covering of a set $s\subseteq M$. Then $\sum_\alpha f_\alpha(p)=1$ for every
  $p\in s$.
