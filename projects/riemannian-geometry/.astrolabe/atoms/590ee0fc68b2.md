title: Leibniz rule for the bracket
---
For smooth vector fields $X,Y$ and smooth functions $f,g$ one has
  $[fX,gY]=fg[X,Y]+fX(g)Y-gY(f)X$, where $X(g)=dg_p(X_p)$ denotes the derivative
  of $g$ along $X$.
