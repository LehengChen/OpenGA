title: uniqueness of the invariant geodesic
---
If $K<0$ and $f:\tilde{M}\to\tilde{M}$ is a translation along the geodesic
  $\tilde{\gamma}$, $f\neq\mathrm{id}$, then $\tilde{\gamma}$ is the unique geodesic
  left invariant by $f$.
