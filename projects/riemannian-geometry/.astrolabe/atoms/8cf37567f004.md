title: linearity properties of $R$
---
The curvature $R$ of a Riemannian manifold has the following properties:
  - (i) $R$ is bilinear in $\mathcal{X}(M)\times\mathcal{X}(M)$, that is,
  
  $$
  R(fX_1+gX_2,Y_1)=fR(X_1,Y_1)+gR(X_2,Y_1),
  $$
  
  $$
  R(X_1,fY_1+gY_2)=fR(X_1,Y_1)+gR(X_1,Y_2),\quad f,g\in\mathcal{D}(M),\ X_1,X_2,Y_1,Y_2\in\mathcal{X}(M).
  $$
  
  - (ii) For any $X,Y\in\mathcal{X}(M)$, the curvature operator
  $R(X,Y):\mathcal{X}(M)\to\mathcal{X}(M)$ is linear, that is,
  
  $$
  R(X,Y)(Z+W)=R(X,Y)Z+R(X,Y)W,\qquad R(X,Y)fZ=fR(X,Y)Z,\quad f\in\mathcal{D}(M),\ Z,W\in\mathcal{X}(M).
  $$
