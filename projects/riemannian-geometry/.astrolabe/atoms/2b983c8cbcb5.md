title: $d$ is a metric
---
With the distance $d$, $M$ is a metric space, that is:
  1. $d(p,r)\le d(p,q)+d(q,r)$,
  2. $d(p,q)=d(q,p)$,
  3. $d(p,q)\ge 0$, and $d(p,q)=0\iff p=q$.
