title: 'uniqueness: a vector field is determined by its action on functions'
---
A tangent vector at $p$, and hence a vector field on $M$, is determined by its
  action on differentiable real functions: if $Z_1,Z_2$ are sections of $TM$ with
  $Z_1f=Z_2f$ for every $f\in\mathcal{D}$, then $Z_1=Z_2$. This is the uniqueness
  clause of \cref{lem:dc-ch0-5-2}: at most one field acts as $(XY-YX)$.
