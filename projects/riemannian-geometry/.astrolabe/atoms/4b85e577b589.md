title: differentiable but not an immersion

  $\alpha:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}^2$, $\alpha(t)=(t^3,t^2)$, is differentiable but
  not an immersion: the immersion condition is $\alpha'(t)\neq0$, which fails at
  $t=0$ (Fig. 6).
