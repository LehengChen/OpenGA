title: invariant vector of an orthogonal map
---
Let $A$ be an orthogonal linear transformation of $\mathbf{R}^{n-1}$ and suppose
  that $\det A=(-1)^n$. Then $A$ leaves invariant some non-zero vector of
  $\mathbf{R}^{n-1}$.
