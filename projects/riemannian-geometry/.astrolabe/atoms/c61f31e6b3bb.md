An analysis of the proof above shows that the necessity of the appearance of the
  term $\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z$ in the definition of the curvature is connected to the fact
  that we want the mapping $R(X,Y):\mathcal{X}(M)\to\mathcal{X}(M)$ to be linear
  (see the next \cref{rem:dc-ch4-2-6}).
