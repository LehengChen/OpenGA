Let $p\in M$ and $v\in T_pM$ with $\exp_p v$ defined, and $w\in T_pM\approx T_v(T_pM)$.
  Then
  
  $$
  \langle(d\exp_p)_v(v),\,(d\exp_p)_v(w)\rangle=\langle v,w\rangle.\qquad\text{(2)}
  $$
