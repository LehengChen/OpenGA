title: positive sectional curvature bound
---
Let $M$ be a complete Riemannian manifold with sectional curvature
  $K\ge\frac{1}{r^2}>0$. Then $M$ is compact, $\mathrm{diam}(M)\le\pi r$ and
  $\pi_1(M)$ is finite.
