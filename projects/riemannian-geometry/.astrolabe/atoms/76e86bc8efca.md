title: differential of a diffeomorphism is an isomorphism
---
If $\varphi:M_1\to M_2$ is a diffeomorphism then for every $p\in M_1$ the
  differential $d\varphi_p:T_pM_1\to T_{\varphi(p)}M_2$ is a linear isomorphism.
