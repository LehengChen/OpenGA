Let $J$ be a Jacobi field along the geodesic $\gamma:[0,a]\to M$. Then
  
  $$
  \langle J(t),\gamma'(t)\rangle=\langle J'(0),\gamma'(0)\rangle t+\langle J(0),\gamma'(0)\rangle,\quad t\in[0,a].
  $$
