If $M$ is compact, $K<0$, and $H$ is a solvable subgroup of $\pi_1(M)$,
  $H\neq\{e\}$, then $H$ is infinite cyclic. In addition, $\pi_1(M)$ does not have a
  cyclic subgroup of finite index.
