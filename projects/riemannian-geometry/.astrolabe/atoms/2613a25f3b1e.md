title: exp_q is a local diffeomorphism
---
Given $q\in M$, there exists $\varepsilon>0$ such that
  $\exp_q:B_\varepsilon(0)\subset T_qM\to M$ is a diffeomorphism of
  $B_\varepsilon(0)$ onto an open subset of $M$.
