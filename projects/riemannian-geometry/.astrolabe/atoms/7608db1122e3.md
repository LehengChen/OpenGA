  Let $M$ be a compact Riemannian manifold and $p,q\in M$ with
  $d(p,q)=\operatorname{diam}M$. Then for all $W\in T_pM$ there exists a minimizing
  geodesic $\gamma$ from $p=\gamma(0)$ to $q$ with $\langle\gamma'(0),W\rangle\geq0$.
